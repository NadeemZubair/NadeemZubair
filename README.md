👨‍🔬 About Me

I am an **Agricultural Engineer and water-resources researcher** with expertise in **GIS, remote sensing, irrigation engineering, hydrological analysis, environmental modelling, and spatial data analysis**.

My research focuses on the interaction between **agriculture, water resources, climate, land-use change, and environmental systems**, with an emphasis on developing data-driven approaches for sustainable resource management.

I am particularly interested in combining:

**Agricultural Engineering + GIS + Remote Sensing + Data Science + Machine Learning**

to address real-world agricultural and environmental challenges.

- LULC classification
